The Artist

Russell Strand has been creating fine art for about a decade with a sharp focus on the built environment. He considers himself a lifelong self-directed student of drawing and painting and has been informed by the diverse and beautiful people he has met and places he has discovered, as well as leading painting workshops around the North Coast, studying sculpture and drafting at Lane Community College, and during his many jobs as a trades laborer. Russell's work has been featured at/in the Hoffman Center for the Arts, North Coast Squid, Oregon State University, Eugene Mayor's Art Show, and the Nehalem Bay Health Center.
